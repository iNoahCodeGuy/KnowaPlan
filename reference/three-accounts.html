The three accounts — field guide

Every KnowaPlan charge moves money between three parties. A refund touches two of them. The third is where corrections go wrong. Print this.

The one rule A refund makes the cardholder whole out of your platform balance. It does not touch the connected account. To pull the transferred money back, you must reverse the transfer — a separate action.

Who's who

AccountWho it isWhat lives here
Cardholder The attendee Pays the share. A refund returns money here. Always visible, always works.
Platform You (KnowaPlan) Charge created here → lands here first. Stripe's fee debited here. Refunds paid from here. Goes negative (Lesson 2).
Connected The planner (you, solo) Destination of transfer_data. Gets the full share the instant the charge succeeds. A refund leaves it untouched.

What each action touches

ActionPlatform (you)ConnectedCardholder
Charge $X +$X, −fee, −$X out +$X −$X
Refund $X −$X paid out untouched +$X (whole)
Reverse transfer +$X back −$X → $0 n/a

The fee never comes back. A refunded charge always costs you the 2.9% + 30¢, reversal or not.

Two facts that will fool you

1 — A refund alone reads as “done” The cardholder is whole, the dashboard shows refunded, and solo it nets to the same bottom line whether or not you reverse. So skipping the reversal feels complete. It isn't — the transferred dollars are stranded in the connected account.
2 — “Harmless” expires the moment there's a second planner Solo, both platform and connected are your pockets, so a stranded transfer is bookkeeping. With another planner on the connected account, a refund-without-reversal pays your refund out of the shared platform while the money sits in their account.

The correction reflex (7/29)

Settlement is one-shot — no in-app re-mark (decisions.md). A wrong charge, or a walk-in that lowers a share after a charge landed, is corrected only in the dashboard:

1. Refund Returns the share to the card, out of your platform balance.
2. Reverse Set reverse_transfer: true at refund time, or reverse the transfer after the fact. Pulls the money out of the connected account.
3. Read Confirm the transfer shows reversed: true. That — not the refund — is what says the correction fully landed.
Does KnowaPlan know? No. A dashboard refund happens outside the app — refund_charge is a guarded stub until walk-ins ship (payment-row guide). The Payment row will still read paid. Reconcile by hand until the refund path is authored.

Source of truth: Stripe Refund and cancel payments (reverse_transfer default; connected account “retains the funds by default”) · Fees for refunded payments (fees “not returned”) · Create destination charges.
